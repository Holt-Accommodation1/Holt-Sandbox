Important

These terms apply to accommodation supplied by Holt Accommodation Solutions Limited to companies and other organisations for their employees, contractors and other authorised occupants.

The client is the company or organisation making the booking and is responsible for payment and for ensuring that its occupants comply with these terms and any property-specific requirements.

Holt supplies accommodation in its own name, whether the property is owned by Holt or provided through one of our accommodation partners.

1. Your booking

The written quotation or booking confirmation will set out the property, dates, permitted occupancy, price, payment arrangements, included services, deposit requirements and any specific cancellation or notice terms.

These agreed booking details take priority over these standard terms. Any separately signed agreement will take priority where its terms differ.

A booking becomes binding when the client instructs Holt to proceed and Holt confirms the booking in writing. These terms form part of the booking where they have been provided to the client, or made available by a link, with the quotation or booking confirmation.

Where the client instructs Holt to proceed and Holt commits to accommodation or incurs costs in reliance on that instruction, the client is responsible for those commitments in accordance with the agreed booking terms.

An enquiry or provisional discussion does not reserve accommodation unless Holt confirms otherwise in writing.

The person confirming the booking must have authority to act on behalf of the client.

Any subsequent changes must be agreed in writing. Email confirmation is sufficient.

2. Price and payment

Accommodation charges are payable in accordance with the payment schedule agreed for the booking. This may include payment monthly, every 28 days, for a longer period in advance or on another agreed basis.

The booking confirmation will set out the applicable charges, VAT and any taxes or statutory levies payable.

If a new tax, visitor levy or other statutory charge becomes applicable to the accommodation after the booking is confirmed, or an existing statutory charge increases, Holt may add the amount legally payable to the accommodation charges.

Invoices must be paid by the agreed due date.

Where a booking has been properly authorised, the absence or delay of a purchase order does not remove the client's obligation to pay.

Holt may charge statutory interest and recovery costs on overdue business debts where permitted by law.

Where payment is overdue, Holt may decline extensions or additional bookings and may take reasonable and lawful steps to recover outstanding sums or bring the arrangement to an end.

Nothing in these terms permits Holt to unlawfully exclude an occupant from accommodation.

3. Duration, cancellation and notice

Fixed bookings are payable for the agreed booking period unless the booking confirmation provides otherwise or Holt agrees a change in writing.

For longer-term rolling bookings, Holt's standard requirement is two calendar months' written notice, unless a different notice period has been agreed.

Notice should identify the relevant property and the intended final date. Charges continue until the agreed end date.

An early departure, change of shift pattern, reduction in personnel, empty room or no-show does not automatically reduce the agreed accommodation charge.

Holt may, at its discretion, agree an earlier release, reduction or credit in writing, taking account of costs already incurred, contractual commitments and any replacement booking secured.

Any statutory rights that cannot lawfully be excluded remain unaffected.

4. Extensions and booking changes

Extensions, additional rooms, changes of dates or changes in occupancy require Holt's written agreement and may result in a change to the price.

Where the same client continues to occupy the same property under consecutive bookings, a change in individual occupants does not by itself end or reset the accommodation arrangement.

Extensions cannot be guaranteed until confirmed by Holt.

The client must provide accurate occupant details and notify Holt of relevant changes in sufficient time to allow access arrangements, safety requirements and property obligations to be managed properly.

5. Occupants and property rules

The client must ensure that all occupants receive and comply with any arrival information, house rules, safety information and property-specific instructions provided by Holt.

Accommodation may only be used for the agreed purpose and within the confirmed occupancy limit.

Unauthorised overnight guests, subletting, transfer of the accommodation to another person or organisation, unauthorised parties or events, illegal activity, indoor smoking or vaping, serious or persistent disturbance and interference with fire or safety equipment are not permitted.

Pets require Holt's prior written approval and are not normally permitted in corporate accommodation.

Occupants must behave reasonably towards neighbours and comply with any quiet hours or local restrictions applicable to the property.

Occupants must take reasonable care of the property, furnishings, appliances, keys and access systems.

Property-specific house rules may also cover matters such as refuse and recycling, cleaning, parking, heating, security, checkout requirements and use of facilities. Failure to follow these requirements may result in reasonable additional charges where this causes extra cleaning, waste removal, damage, contractor attendance or other costs.

6. Check-in, services and utilities

Check-in and checkout times, parking arrangements, access instructions and arrangements for the collection or return of keys will be provided for the relevant property.

Any additional time, services or changes requested by the client must be agreed in advance and may incur an additional charge.

The booking confirmation will state the cleaning, linen, Wi-Fi, utilities and other services included within the agreed price.

Additional services, exceptional cleaning or usage above an expressly agreed fair-use allowance may be charged separately where reasonable.

Internet, utilities and other services supplied by third parties may occasionally be interrupted for reasons outside Holt's reasonable control. Any fault or interruption must be reported to Holt promptly so that appropriate action can be taken with the relevant supplier, property owner or contractor.

Holt will take reasonable steps to have reported service issues resolved but cannot guarantee uninterrupted third-party services.

7. Damage, deposits and additional costs

Where a security deposit is required, Holt may apply it towards unpaid charges or reasonable losses and costs resulting from the actions of the client, its occupants or their visitors.

This may include damage beyond reasonable wear and tear, missing items, additional cleaning, smoking-related cleaning, lost keys, replacement locks or access systems, unauthorised occupants or visitors, substantiated nuisance, and reasonable contractor or call-out charges.

Any reported damage, nuisance or breach will be considered on the available information before a deduction is made. Where a deduction is made, Holt will provide a reasonable explanation and supporting information where available.

Any remaining deposit balance will be returned within a reasonable period after departure once the property has been checked and any outstanding costs or issues have been established.

Where reasonable and evidenced costs exceed the amount of any deposit held, the balance remains payable by the client.

8. Safety, faults and access

Holt will comply with its applicable legal responsibilities and will take reasonable care in the sourcing and management of accommodation.

Occupants must comply with fire and safety instructions, keep escape routes clear, not interfere with alarms or other safety equipment, and promptly report accidents, hazards, damage, faults or maintenance concerns.

Holt must be given a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address any reported issue.

Non-emergency repairs, alterations or contractor appointments must not be arranged by the client or occupants without Holt's prior approval.

Holt, the property owner or authorised contractors may require reasonable access for cleaning, inspection, maintenance, repair, safety checks or other property-management purposes. Reasonable notice will normally be given.

Immediate access may be required in an emergency or where there is a serious concern regarding safety, damage or security.

All access will be managed in accordance with applicable legal and occupancy rights.

9. If accommodation becomes unavailable

Occasionally, circumstances may mean that the originally booked accommodation becomes unavailable or unsuitable.

In those circumstances, Holt will use reasonable efforts to provide suitable alternative accommodation, taking account of the material requirements of the original booking, including location, size, facilities, occupancy and the client's operational needs.

Where Holt offers alternative accommodation which reasonably meets those material requirements, the booking will continue on the agreed terms, subject to any appropriate adjustment agreed between the parties.

If a client chooses not to accept a reasonably suitable alternative for reasons unrelated to a material difference in the accommodation, this will not automatically cancel the client's existing booking obligations. Holt may treat the client's decision as a cancellation and the agreed cancellation or notice terms will continue to apply.

Where a proposed alternative is materially different from the accommodation originally booked, Holt will discuss the available options with the client and seek a reasonable solution.

Holt will refund or credit payments relating to accommodation that it is unable to supply if it cannot provide suitable alternative accommodation.

Nothing in this section excludes any legal right or remedy that cannot lawfully be restricted.

10. Serious breaches and circumstances outside our control

Where there is serious damage, dangerous or unlawful behaviour, threatening or abusive conduct, significant nuisance, unauthorised occupation, repeated material breaches of property rules or material non-payment, Holt may require the breach to be remedied immediately and may, where lawful, terminate the booking or require occupants to leave.

Any action will be taken in accordance with applicable notice, occupancy and housing laws.

The client remains responsible for outstanding charges and for reasonable losses or costs arising from a breach of these terms.

Neither party will be responsible for a delay or failure to perform an obligation where this is caused by circumstances genuinely outside its reasonable control and reasonable steps are taken to minimise the effect.

This does not remove the client's obligation to pay for accommodation already supplied, booked or committed to in accordance with the agreed terms. A change to a client's project, staffing levels, shift patterns, travel arrangements or operational requirements does not by itself give the client a right to cancel or reduce a booking without charge.

Where circumstances outside Holt's reasonable control prevent Holt from supplying the booked accommodation, section 9 will apply.

11. Responsibility, belongings and liability

The client is responsible for the conduct of its occupants and for reasonable costs or losses resulting from their breach of these terms.

Holt remains responsible for its own contractual obligations and negligence to the extent required by law.

Personal possessions, business equipment, vehicles and other items brought to or left at the accommodation remain the responsibility of their owner. The client and its occupants are responsible for arranging any insurance they consider appropriate.

Holt does not provide insurance for occupants' possessions, vehicles or business equipment and will not be responsible for loss or damage to them except where caused by Holt's negligence or where liability cannot otherwise lawfully be excluded.

To the extent permitted by law and where reasonable in the circumstances of the booking, Holt will not be liable for loss of profit, loss of business, business interruption or other indirect business losses.

Holt's total liability arising from an individual booking will, where legally valid and reasonable, be limited to the accommodation charges paid or payable for that booking.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, fraudulent misrepresentation or any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

12. Information, complaints and legal terms

Holt may use and share information relating to the client and occupants with property owners, accommodation partners, cleaners, maintenance providers and other relevant service providers where this is lawful and reasonably necessary to arrange or manage the accommodation.

The client is responsible for ensuring that it is entitled to provide Holt with the personal information of its employees, contractors or other occupants for the purposes of arranging and managing their accommodation.

Further information is contained in Holt's Privacy Notice.

Any concern or complaint should be reported promptly so that Holt has a reasonable opportunity to investigate and respond. Holt's published Complaints Procedure provides further information.

Formal notices may be sent by email to the contact details used for the booking.

These terms may be amended from time to time for future bookings. Changes will not retrospectively alter a booking already agreed unless both parties agree.

For accommodation situated in Scotland, the booking will be governed by Scots law and, unless otherwise agreed, the Scottish courts will have jurisdiction.

For accommodation situated in England or Wales, the booking will be governed by the law of England and Wales and, unless otherwise agreed, the courts of England and Wales will have jurisdiction.

Any mandatory legal requirements applying to the property or occupancy will continue to apply.

If any provision of these terms is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue to apply.
